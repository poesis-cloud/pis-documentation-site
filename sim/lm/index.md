# LM

{: .no_toc }

> **In construction.** This product is at an early stage; its documentation will follow its first release.

**LM** is the SIM language model. It explores whether language models trained against **[GSM]({% link gsm/index.md %})** definitions and **[SIE]({% link sie/index.md %})** governed context perform better than with any other context. No model is released yet.
